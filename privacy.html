GOOD TO KNOW

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Wigachu is a product-discovery website. You can browse the catalogue without creating an account. This page explains the main ways the site and its service providers may handle information when you use it.

Information stored in your browser

If you save products, Wigachu stores the saved product IDs in your browser's local storage so they remain available on that device. These saved finds are not sent to Wigachu as a user profile. Clearing your browser storage can remove them.

Analytics and site performance

Wigachu uses Meta Pixel to measure page visits and selected actions, such as opening a product, sharing a link, and clicking through to a retailer. Meta may receive information through its own technologies according to your settings and Meta's policies. The website also loads third-party resources such as fonts, icons, and the Supabase JavaScript client; those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests their resources.

Product catalogue, link reports and wholesale requests

The product catalogue is served through Supabase. If you report a broken seller link, the site may send the relevant product ID, seller link, and seller name to its database so the link can be checked. If you submit a wholesale request, the site sends the product's title, image URL, description and listed price. The current request form does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Retailer and affiliate links

When you follow a seller link, you leave Wigachu and visit a third-party website. Retailers and affiliate networks may use cookies or other tracking technologies to attribute a referral or qualifying purchase. Their privacy terms and checkout policies apply on their sites. Wigachu does not control what those third parties collect.

How to manage your choices

You can remove saved finds by unsaving them or clearing local storage for this site. You can also use your browser or device settings to manage cookies and restrict third-party tracking. Some functions, such as remembering saved finds or measuring marketing performance, may work differently if you block storage or tracking.
